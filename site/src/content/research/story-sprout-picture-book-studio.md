summary: >-
  Story Sprout is a browser-based picture-book studio for children, built around an
  explicit allocation of agency that is enforced in the system itself. Children originate
  every sentence of the story and every illustrated scene detail; the model's role is
  confined to drawing the illustration and redrawing it when the child asks for changes.
  After each generation the system asks the child whether the picture matches what they
  imagined — it never makes that judgment for them. The paper presents the agency
  framework, the deployed system, its layered safety mechanism, the aspects of the
  allocation that could be contested, and how the design was shaped by classroom
  workshops.
