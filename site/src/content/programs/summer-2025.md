Over eight sessions, young authors moved from an introduction to illustrated
storytelling, through writing and fixing the descriptions that produce a picture,
on to keeping an art style consistent across a whole book, and finally to shaping
the story itself.

Children wrote the story in their own words. When it came time to illustrate,
they described the scene they had in mind, looked carefully at what the AI
illustrator produced, and revised their description until the picture matched
the story they meant to tell. Learning to say clearly what you want to see is
the skill the sessions were built around.
